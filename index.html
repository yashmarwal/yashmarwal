In Brief

Yash Marwal is a New Delhi entrepreneur working to digitalise and modernise Indian B2B businesses through three ventures: Phaseline, a software and systems company; Optimeta, an AI growth platform for manufacturers and exporters; and Banao Buzz, a GST billing and business toolkit for small businesses.

Ventures

PORTFOLIO DISPATCHES
LAUNCHED DEPT: B2B SOFTWARE

Phaseline

Custom software, business systems, and AI-powered tools for Indian B2B. Phaseline gives manufacturers, traders, and exporters the digital infrastructure to run, sell, and scale on one connected stack, and supports them after launch.

TARGET SECTOR: INDIAN B2B BUSINESSES
IN DEVELOPMENT DEPT: B2B GROWTH

Optimeta

An AI-powered operating system for Indian B2B sales. Optimeta builds a business's online presence, tracks every requirement through an eight-stage pipeline, and drafts replies and quotes, following each inquiry through to payment so nothing lives in WhatsApp, Excel, and memory.

TARGET SECTOR: MANUFACTURING, SUPPLY, EXPORT/TRADING
IN DEVELOPMENT DEPT: BUSINESS SOFTWARE

Banao Buzz

A GST billing and business toolkit for small Indian businesses, offered through Phaseline. Twenty-five features cover sales, purchases and stock, parties and leads, and staff, salary and reports. The owner sets up once, and one tested calculation engine handles the GST split, totals, and numbering.

TARGET SECTOR: SMALL INDIAN BUSINESSES

The Profile

THE FOUNDER

Yash Marwal: a Delhi entrepreneur building three ventures to modernise Indian B2B

Through Phaseline, Optimeta, and Banao Buzz, he is bringing software, systems, and AI to the manufacturers, exporters, and traders at the centre of India's B2B economy.

A PROFILE • NEW DELHI

Yash Marwal is an entrepreneur based in New Delhi. His work centres on one problem: much of Indian B2B commerce, from manufacturing to export and trading, still runs on paper, phone calls, and informal channels. He builds ventures that bring these businesses online and equip them with modern systems.

Phaseline builds software and systems for Indian B2B businesses. Optimeta is an AI platform for Indian B2B sales: it builds a business's online presence, tracks every requirement through an eight-stage pipeline, and drafts replies and quotes. Banao Buzz is a GST billing and business toolkit for small Indian businesses.

He works from New Delhi and writes about what he is building on LinkedIn.
